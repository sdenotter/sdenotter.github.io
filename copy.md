## Philosophy

**Section label:** Brand Pillars

**Heading:**
> The non-negotiable truths of the craft.

### 01 — Diagnosis First
Understand before you build. Scott's first question is never 'what do you want built?' It's 'what's actually going wrong?' The diagnostic process is the product, not a free add-on.

### 02 — Simplicity as Craft
The simplest working solution is the best solution. It takes more skill to resist over-engineering than to indulge it. Scott regularly talks clients down from complexity they don't need.

### 03 — Outcome First
Clients don't buy code. They buy results. Every project is framed around what changed for the business — time saved, errors eliminated, decisions made faster.

### 04 — Honest Expertise
Be the expert who tells the truth. Credibility comes from candour, not credentials. Scott understands the craft deeply enough to teach it, which provides proof of expertise.
