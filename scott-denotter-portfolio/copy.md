## Philosophy

<!-- src/components/Philosophy.tsx:66 -->
**Section label:** Brand Pillars

<!-- src/components/Philosophy.tsx:68-70 -->

> The non-negotiable truths of my craft.

<!-- src/components/Philosophy.tsx:15-29 (principles array, index 0) -->
### 01 — Diagnosis First
I need to understand before I build. My first question is never "what do you want built?" It's "what's actually going wrong?" To me, the diagnostic process is the core product, not just a free add-on.

<!-- src/components/Philosophy.tsx:15-29 (principles array, index 1) -->
### 02 — Simplicity as Craft
The simplest working solution is always the best solution. It takes more skill to resist over-engineering than to indulge it, so I frequently work with clients to cut away complexity they don't actually need.

<!-- src/components/Philosophy.tsx:15-29 (principles array, index 2) -->
### 03 — Outcome First
You aren't buying code; you're buying results. I frame every project around what actually changes for your business—whether that's time saved, costly errors eliminated, or decisions made faster.

<!-- src/components/Philosophy.tsx:15-29 (principles array, index 3) -->
### 04 — Honest Expertise
I strive to be the expert who tells the truth. Credibility comes from candour, not just credentials. I understand the craft deeply enough to teach it, and I bring that transparency into every client partnership.
